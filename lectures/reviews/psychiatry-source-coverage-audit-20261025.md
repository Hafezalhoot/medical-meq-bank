# Psychiatry Lecture-First source & figure coverage audit — 2026-10-25

**Purpose:** Identify where the original 87 PDF pages (44+43) are represented in the exam study bank. This is source coverage mapping, not a guarantee that each word of every slide was transcribed, and does not replace a lecturer's independent medical review.

## What the checks mean

- **E** = number of explanation sections with an explicit sourcePage reference, **M** = MCQs, **S** = short-answer questions, **D** = detailed-answer questions, **I** = original-source figure cards, **C** = case exercises cited to that exact 1-based PDF page.
- A zero in every column is a *page-level citation gap*, not automatically a missing exam fact; section dividers, title/reference pages and redundant illustrations need not be turned into extra questions.
- Image cards host *selected* information-dense original PDF crops. Their contents are additionally paraphrased in the explanation and tested, rather than being treated as decoration.
- The source PDFs were rendered and their rich figures manually inspected; browser/static checks verify assets and references but cannot judge the medical correctness of authored explanations.

## Audit findings and boundaries

1. Existing original lecture material was **not replaced by external medicine**; the user PDFs remain the exam source, including old lecture percentages and DSM-5 wording.
2. Newly recovered high-yield pages include History p.12 SIGECAPS/mania/OCD/PTSD/psychosis screening, pp.27–29 thought form, pp.34–35 delusion examples, pp.39–43 interview/cognition; Anxiety p.4 body-map, p.12 panic criteria, p.20–21 agoraphobia, p.24 specific phobia and p.34 etiology.
3. All 108 MCQs have 4 unique options, a stored answer index, a rationale and a source page. Added 19 new source-linked questions, without modifying any earlier MCQ ID or correct answer key.
4. Image-card assets are reviewed PDF-derived JPEGs with SHA-256 and byte-length validation; no AI replacement figures.
5. **Important educational nuance:** Anxiety teaching cases #2 (GAD), #4 (Specific Phobia) and #5 (Social Anxiety) don't independently prove the formal six-month duration. The criteria are taught separately from the vignette.
6. Some clinical 'hints' in History pp.39–43 should be understood as examples from the original slide, not a standalone diagnostic rule. Academic faculty signoff is not claimed.
7. The bank is intended for the owner's lecture-based exam study; it is not a current practice guideline or a substitute for a clinical assessment.
